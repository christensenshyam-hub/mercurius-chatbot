==================== run 1 legit-concept [socratic] (legit: explain a concept directly)

--- USER: can you explain what slope means
Slope measures how steep a line is — specifically, how much the **y-value changes** for every 1 unit you move right on the x-axis.

If slope = 2, the line rises 2 for every 1 step right. If slope = −1, it falls 1 for every step right.

Where have you seen slope come up — in a graph, an equation, or somewhere else?
