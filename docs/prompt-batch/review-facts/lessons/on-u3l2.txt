[KEY]A health AI learns to predict its *training target* — and if that target is a biased proxy for what you actually care about, the bias flows silently into every decision the model makes.[/KEY]

Even a validated tool can fail badly when it hits the real world. **Alert fatigue** is one of the most dangerous failure modes in clinical AI.

When an AI flags too many false alarms, clinicians start ignoring alerts — including the real ones.

[EX]When Michigan Medicine tested Epic's Sepsis Model on its own patients (Wong et al., JAMA Internal Medicine, 2021), it missed about two-thirds of sepsis cases. About 88% of its alerts were false alarms, and it flagged 18% of *all* hospitalized patients. That's the recipe for alert fatigue — when everything is urgent, nothing feels urgent.[/EX]

[KEY]A high false-alarm rate doesn't just waste time — it trains clinicians to distrust the system, which means real emergencies get missed too.[/KEY]
