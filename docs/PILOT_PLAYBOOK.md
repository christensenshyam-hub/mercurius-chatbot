# Pilot playbook — Mayo AI Literacy Club, fall 2026

This is the run-book for the 8-week club pilot of Mercurius AI. It is
written for me, to be read in the room on Thursday and at the desk on
Friday. Nothing here is a goal I hope for; every number is a threshold
with an action attached.

The deal in one paragraph: about 25 club members install the app on
kickoff day, do two lessons a week for eight weeks, and tell me what
confused them and what Merc got wrong. I read the server's numbers every
Friday, fix one thing a week, and do not build anything new until the
app has held 15 weekly users for three weeks straight. It is free, there
are no accounts, and anyone can delete their data from Settings.

Companions: [`APP_STORE_LISTING.md`](APP_STORE_LISTING.md) (what the app
promises), [`REVIEW_REHEARSAL.md`](REVIEW_REHEARSAL.md) (the on-device
walk-through), [`LOAD_REHEARSAL.md`](LOAD_REHEARSAL.md) (the server drill),
[`DEPLOY.md`](DEPLOY.md) (Railway variables).

Product facts this playbook relies on (do not promise anything else in the
room): three modes (Socratic, Discussion, Debate); a path of 8 units and 35
lessons, streamed in beats with tappable KEY IDEA / EXAMPLE / CHECK cards;
a lesson is marked complete when the student demonstrates the objective,
not when they reach the end; a unit check after each unit (a short
multiple-choice quiz over the unit plus one written answer); streaks and an
optional daily reminder; ages 13+; "Delete my data & start over" in
Settings; long-press any reply to report it with a reason. Every reply is
generated by Anthropic's Claude through our server.

## 1. Kickoff script — Thursday, October 8, 2026, 8:20 AM

Bring: the printed poster (two copies), my phone with the app already
installed and a real streak, my laptop open to the Railway logs and the
Discord alerts channel, and the captains' names written down. Prewarm the
prompt cache at 8:10 and again at 8:26 (section 9). No deploys this
morning.

**8:20 — Phones out (1 min).**
Say: "This is the app the club built. It's a tutor for how AI works. It
asks you questions instead of giving you answers, which is the point. We
are going to install it, do the first lesson right now, and then the deal
is two short lessons a week. That's it."

**8:21 — The poster / QR moment (2 min).**
Hold up the poster. Say: "One link, forever: trymercurius.com/get. It's
on the poster, it's on the door, it's pinned in the group chat. Right now
it opens TestFlight — that's Apple's beta app — because our App Store
version is in review. If your phone asks you to install TestFlight first,
do that, then come back to the link. When the store version is approved,
the same link goes to the App Store and your progress carries over. Same
QR, no new link."

Show: scan the QR with my own phone camera so they see it works.

**8:23 — Install (4 min).**
The Install captain walks the room. Common snags: Safari asks to open
TestFlight (say yes); TestFlight not installed (App Store → TestFlight →
back to the link); "Accept" then "Install"; iOS 16 or older will not
install (the app needs iOS 17). Anyone without an iPhone pairs up with a
neighbor today and is not on the hook this week.

While they install, say what they are about to see so nobody is surprised:
"Four screens before the first lesson. Meet Merc — that's just the mascot.
Then 'How old are you?' — the app is 13 and up; your age stays on your
phone and is not sent anywhere. Then a screen called 'Before you start':
it tells you exactly what happens with what you type — your messages, and
any photo you attach, are sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate the
reply. There's a checkbox that says that in one sentence; read it, then
Agree. If you'd rather not, tap Not now and the app just waits — nothing
is sent. Then 'What Merc can't do': it can be wrong, it is not a person,
and it is not for emergencies — that screen has the 988 line on it. Then
your path, Lesson 1."

**8:27 — Everyone opens the app (3 min).**
Walk it together, one screen at a time: Meet Merc → Continue → age →
Continue → Before you start: read the checkbox line out loud, tick it →
Agree and continue → What Merc can't do → Got it → Your path.

On the path screen: "There's a daily reminder toggle. It's off by
default. If you turn it on, pick a time you actually have your phone in
your hand — Merc will nudge you at that time each day, and stops on its
own if you stop opening the app. Your call." Then: "Tap Start Lesson 1."

**8:30 — Three silent minutes.**
Say: "Three minutes, no talking. Read, answer Merc, get to the first
CHECK card. It's a conversation — type like you'd text." Watch the
Railway log scroll; that is the room's traffic. If the log shows 503s,
section 9 has the two things to check (kill switch, budget).

**8:33 — One volunteer reads their KEY IDEA card (2 min).**
Ask one person to tap their KEY IDEA card and read it out. Ask one
question back to the room: "Does that match what you thought happened
when you type a prompt?" Thirty seconds of answers, then move on.

**8:35 — The deal and the three ways to talk to me (3 min).**
Say: "Two lessons a week. Finish Lesson 1 today or tonight; Lesson 2 by
Sunday. Next Thursday is MEA, no meeting — the deal still stands, Lessons
3 and 4 by that Sunday, and you get a four-day weekend to do it in."

"Three ways to tell me things. One: the feedback form — QR on the poster,
two minutes, three questions, no name. Two: the group chat, for 'it's
broken' only. Three: inside the app, long-press any reply and tap Report,
pick a reason — I read every one of those on Friday."

Name the captains: "Install captain is ___ — go to them this week if the
app won't install. Feedback captain is ___ — they own the form. Streak
captain is ___ — if you want your streak shouted out, tell them; it's
opt-in, first names only, and there is no leaderboard in the app."

**8:38 — Close (1 min).**
Say: "No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your session is a random id.
Settings has 'Delete my data & start over' and it does exactly that. See
you in two weeks — Lesson 2 by Sunday."

Done by 8:40. Before leaving the room: count installs by a show of hands
and write the number down. That is W1's first data point and it feeds the
first decision rule (section 6).

## 2. The weekly ritual

### The card (5 minutes, every Thursday, read it as written)

1. **Phones out.** "Open Mercurius." (30 s)
2. **Home → Start learning.** The path opens on your next lesson — the one
   marked **Start now** (or **In progress**). "Tap it." (30 s)
3. **Three silent minutes.** "Get to the first CHECK card. No talking." (3 min)
4. **One volunteer reads their KEY IDEA card.** Pick someone who has not
   read before. (1 min)
5. **Close.** "Second one by Sunday. If your reminder's on, Merc will bug
   you — Wednesday's the one to listen to." (10 s)

Rules for the ritual: never more than five minutes, never a lecture,
never "who hasn't done it". The app derives "this week's lesson" from
each student's own frontier, so someone who is ahead does the next one
and someone who is behind does the one they are on. Nobody is punished for
either; the only thing I ask for is two by Sunday.

Weeks with no meeting (W2, W8): post steps 2 and 5 as one message in the
group chat on Thursday morning. The Streak captain does the KEY IDEA
share by asking one volunteer to paste theirs in the chat.

has done every pair since. The unit check sits on the path right after
the unit's last lesson; it counts as part of that week.

## 5. Friday checklist (30 minutes)

Once, in the Mac shell (never paste the password into chat or a commit):

```bash
export BASE=https://mercurius-chatbot-production.up.railway.app
export ADMIN='<the production ADMIN_PASSWORD>'
```

`jq` makes the JSON readable (`brew install jq` once). If it is missing,
pipe through `python3 -m json.tool` instead.

The daily Discord digest already headlines Thursday by the time I wake up
on Friday (it posts after 13:00 UTC — 8 AM Central in October, 7 AM after
clocks change Nov 1). The curls below are for the 7-day totals and the
per-day rows.

### 5.1 Stats (5 min)

```bash
curl -s -H "x-admin-password: $ADMIN" "$BASE/api/admin/stats?days=7" | jq '{
  wau, newSessions,
  lessonsStarted, lessonsCompleted, lessonsAbandoned,
  costUsdWindow, costPerWau,
  d1: .retention.d1, d7: .retention.d7,
  reportsOpen,
  budget: {usd: .budget.usd, cap: .budget.budgetUsd},
  killSwitch: .killSwitch.disabled,
  topErrors,
  perDay: [.perDay[] | {day, dau, lessonsStarted, lessonsCompleted, costUsd, errors}]
}'
```

Read off, in this order, and write them into the log (section 10):

| Field | What it is | What I do with it |
|---|---|---|
| `wau` | distinct sessions that sent a user message in the last 7 days | The headline. **Subtract my own devices** (my session ids are in Settings). |
| `perDay[]` | one row per UTC day, oldest first; the last row is today and partial | Thursday is `perDay[-2]`. Its `dau` should be about the number of people in the room; if it is far lower, the ritual did not happen on phones. |
| `lessonsStarted` / `lessonsCompleted` | 7-day totals | `lessonsCompleted ÷ wau` is lessons per active student. Target ≥ 2.0; the W3 rule fires under 1.0. |
| `lessonsAbandoned` | lessons opened whose student never answered a second turn within 24 h | `lessonsAbandoned ÷ lessonsStarted` is the "opened it in the room and walked away" rate. Over 30% means the opener is not landing. |
| `costUsdWindow` / `costPerWau` | 7-day Anthropic spend and spend per active student | Target ≤ $1.00 per WAU per week. |
| `retention.d1.rate` / `.d7.rate` (+ `cohortSize`) | fraction of a day's new sessions that came back exactly 1 / 7 days later | Ignore any rate whose `cohortSize` is under 5. The stats' d7 is a single-day return; the week-window version in 5.4 is the one the target uses. |
| `newSessions` | sessions created in the window | Installs this week, plus every "Delete my data & start over", plus me. |
| `reportsOpen` | all-time count of unresolved reports | Must be 0 by the end of this checklist. |
| `budget.usd` vs `budget.budgetUsd` | today's spend against `DAILY_BUDGET_USD` | If Thursday's `costUsd` was over 80% of the cap, raise the cap before next Thursday. |
| `killSwitch.disabled` | must be `false` | If `true`, someone left it on: `POST /api/admin/kill-switch {"disabled":false}` (see REVIEW_REHEARSAL step 11). |
| `topErrors` | the ten most common failures by route | If anything is here with a count over 5, it is a candidate for the one fix. |

Numbers are bucketed by UTC day, not Central; a lesson done at 8 PM
Thursday lands on Friday's row. That is fine for weekly totals — just do
not read per-day rows as school days.

### 5.2 Reports (5 min)

```bash
curl -s -H "x-admin-password: $ADMIN" "$BASE/api/admin/reports?unresolved=1" | jq '.reports[] | {
  id, reason, when: (.created_at / 1000 | floor | todate),
  student_said: (.user_message // "" | .[0:200]),
  merc_said: (.content | .[0:300])
}'
```

For each one: read the student's turn and Merc's reply; decide wrong /
harmful / off topic / noise; note anything that repeats. Then resolve it
so the queue is empty:

```bash
curl -s -X POST -H "x-admin-password: $ADMIN" "$BASE/api/admin/reports/<id>/resolve"
```

Reports per 100 lessons this week (all reports, not just open ones):

```bash
SINCE=$(( $(date +%s) * 1000 - 7 * 86400000 ))
curl -s -H "x-admin-password: $ADMIN" "$BASE/api/admin/reports?since=$SINCE&limit=500" | jq '.reports | length'
# divide by this week's lessonsStarted, times 100
```

Then the form: open the response sheet, read the new rows, and write
down the top confusion and the top "Merc got wrong" in one line each.

### 5.3 Pick ONE fix for Claude (10 min)

One. Write it as a single sentence with its evidence, e.g. "The Unit 1
check's written question confuses people (4 form rows, 2 reports): the
prompt should restate what a passing answer contains." Open it Monday
in a Claude Code session; prompt changes go through
`scripts/eval-pacing.mjs --runs 3` in both `USE_UNIFIED_PROMPT` states
before they merge. If nothing rises above one mention, the fix is
"nothing" and I say so in the log.

Not a fix: anything that adds a feature, a screen, a mode, or content.

### 5.4 The two numbers `/stats` does not have (5 min, from W2 on)

Run in the Railway Postgres console (dashboard → the Postgres service →
Data, or `railway connect`). Both are read-only.

Students at pace this week (two or more distinct lessons completed in the
last 7 days) — divide by `wau`:

```sql
SELECT count(*) AS at_pace FROM (
  SELECT session_id
  FROM lesson_events
  WHERE event = 'complete'
    AND ts >= (extract(epoch from now()) * 1000)::bigint - 7 * 86400000
  GROUP BY session_id
  HAVING count(DISTINCT lesson_id) >= 2
) s;
```

New sessions this week that started Lesson 1 (target ≥ 85%):

```sql
SELECT
  count(*) AS new_sessions,
  count(*) FILTER (WHERE EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM lesson_events le
    WHERE le.session_id = s.session_id AND le.lesson_id = 'u1_l1' AND le.event = 'start'
  )) AS started_lesson_1
FROM sessions s
WHERE s.created_at >= (extract(epoch from now()) * 1000)::bigint - 7 * 86400000;
```

Week-window retention of the W1 installers (sessions created Oct 8–14,
Central). D7 = active any day 7–13 after install (run from Fri Oct 23);
D30 = active any day 28–35 (run from Fri Nov 13). Change the two day
numbers for the other one:

```sql
WITH cohort AS (
  SELECT session_id, created_at FROM sessions
  WHERE (to_timestamp(created_at / 1000.0) AT TIME ZONE 'America/Chicago') >= '2026-10-08'
    AND (to_timestamp(created_at / 1000.0) AT TIME ZONE 'America/Chicago') <  '2026-10-15'
)
SELECT count(*) AS installers,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE EXISTS (
         SELECT 1 FROM messages m
         WHERE m.session_id = c.session_id AND m.role = 'user'
           AND m.timestamp >= c.created_at + 7  * 86400000
           AND m.timestamp <  c.created_at + 14 * 86400000
       )) AS retained
FROM cohort c;
```

A student who taps "Delete my data & start over" gets a new session and
drops out of the cohort; that undercounts retention slightly and is the
price of the promise. Note it, do not correct for it.

## 6. Decision rules

Read on the Friday named; act by the following Thursday. The rule that
fires is the only product decision that week.

| When | If | Then | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri Oct 9 (W1) | show-of-hands installs **< 15** | Add a Monday-lunch install slot (Oct 12, Install captain + me, 20 min). | Do not touch the product. Low installs is a room problem. |
| Fri Oct 23 (W3) | `wau` **< 10** | Next Thursday, finish the whole first lesson of the pair in the room (10 min, not 3). Streak captain posts a reminder in the chat Tuesday and Thursday for the rest of the pilot. | Do not add features to "drive engagement". |
| Fri Oct 23 (W3) | `lessonsCompleted ÷ wau` **< 1.0** | Lessons are too long. Tighten the curriculum prompt's beats and gate the change on `scripts/eval-pacing.mjs --runs 3` (both flag states). | Do not cut lessons from the path or lower the completion bar. |
| Fri Nov 6 (W5) | week-window D7 **≥ 50%** **and** `wau` **≥ 15** | Prepare one second cohort: a teacher's advisory period, same poster, same ritual, same captains model, still student-only. Start it no earlier than Dec 3. | Do not build teacher tools for it. The teacher gets this playbook, not a dashboard. |
| Any Friday | reports this week **> 2 per 100** `lessonsStarted` | Freeze features. The only work until the rate is under 1 is reading reports and fixing what they point at. | Do not ship the point release that week. |
| Any Friday | `killSwitch.disabled` is `true`, or `budget.usd` hit the cap on a school day | Fix the rail before anything else (turn the switch off; raise `DAILY_BUDGET_USD`). | Do not read that week's WAU as a product signal. |

The gate for anything new (section 8) is `wau ≥ 15` for three consecutive
Friday reads, own devices excluded. Earliest possible date: Fri Nov 6.

## 8. What we will NOT do this quarter

Until `wau ≥ 15` for three consecutive Friday reads, none of the
following gets a branch, a mock-up, or a conversation with a teacher:

- Teacher tools of any kind: dashboards, class codes, progress views,
  assignment links.
- Accounts, logins, names, emails, or parent portals. Sessions stay
  anonymous ids.
- New lessons, new units, or rewriting existing lesson content. Fixing a
  lesson that reports or the form say is broken is allowed; that is the
  one fix a week.
- Monetization, sponsorships, or anything that puts a price on a screen.
- A leaderboard, ranks, or any comparison between students inside the app.
- A Discord (or any server) for students. The chat we already have is the
  chat.
- Android, web app parity, or push notifications from the server.
- Product changes in reaction to the W1 install number; that is a room
  problem with a room fix.
- More than one prompt change a week, and never one that skipped the
  pacing eval.
- A second cohort before the W5 gate passes, and never one that is not
  student-only.
