What Mercurius is — and isn't

Mercurius is an educational tool. It's an AI literacy tutor that helps you understand and think critically about artificial intelligence. Its replies are generated by an AI language model (Anthropic's Claude), guided by a tutoring system prompt written by us.

It is not a substitute for a real person. Mercurius is for learning and general information only. It is not a counselor, a doctor, a lawyer, or a therapist, and it does not replace one. Don't rely on it for decisions about your health, your safety, your money, or the law. If something matters, ask a real expert.

If you are in crisis, get real help. Mercurius is instructed to point you to people who can actually help. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text 741741. Both are free and open 24 hours a day.

About the AI's answers

The whole point of Mercurius is to make you a sharper judge of AI, so we hold the app to the same standard we teach:

  • AI can be wrong. Language models sometimes produce confident statements that are inaccurate, outdated, or entirely made up. Treat Mercurius's answers as a starting point, not a final authority.
  • It is told to say when it is unsure. Mercurius is instructed to tell you when it isn't confident, and to have you check claims against a real source before you use them. It will not always get this right, which is exactly why you should check.
  • Verify what matters. For anything you'll act on or hand in, confirm it against a primary or authoritative source. Mercurius is built to encourage exactly this habit.
  • You decide how to use the output. You're responsible for what you do with the replies Mercurius generates.
