Privacy

What we collect — and what we don't.

Mercurius AI teaches AI literacy. Hiding how the app itself handles your data would make a poor lesson. So here is the full picture, in plain language, for students and for parents.

Effective: September 2026 · We update this page whenever the facts change.

Who Mercurius is for

Mercurius AI is built for teens aged 13 to 18. If you are under 13, you may not use it.

Starting with app version 2.3, the app asks for your birth year the first time you open it. If you are under 13, it stops there. Your birth year is not sent to us or stored anywhere — it is only used to answer that one question on your phone.

There are no accounts. You never give us a name, an email address, or a password.

What we collect

A random session ID. When you open the app for the first time, Mercurius generates a 32-character random identifier and stores it in the iOS Keychain on your device. The server sees this ID on every request so it can recognize you across sessions for features like streaks and conversation memory. It is not tied to your name, email, phone number, or any external identity.

Your messages. Every chat message you send, and every lesson transcript, goes to our server and is forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the reply. Messages are stored on our server under your session ID so Mercurius can remember the conversation across launches.

Photos you attach. If you attach a photo to a message (a worksheet, a screenshot), it takes the same path as a message: to our server, then to Anthropic to generate the reply. Photos are deleted from our server after 24 hours.

Content you report. If you report a reply as wrong or harmful, that reply and the message you sent just before it are saved as a report on our server for the developer to review. A notification with the report's number and reason — never the text — goes to a private Discord channel that only the developer reads.

Anonymous usage rows. For each request we record which feature was used, how many tokens it took, and what it cost. These rows contain no message content.

Lesson progress. Which lessons you have started and finished, so your progress is still there after a reinstall.

A hashed network address. Your phone's IP address is scrambled with a secret key before anything is stored. We keep only the scrambled version, never the raw address, and we use it only to protect the service from abuse.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, names, or emails. Mercurius doesn't ask for an account and doesn't have one. No name, email, phone number, or password.
  • No contacts, no location. The app never reads your contacts and never asks for your location.
  • No advertising identifiers. We don't read the device advertising ID, and there are no ads.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs. No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no tracking libraries. None.
  • Nothing is sold. Your messages are not sold, shared for advertising, or used to target you with anything.
  • No device fingerprinting. The session ID is randomly generated, not derived from your device.

What "not linked to you" means in practice

On our server, your conversations sit under a random string of letters and numbers. There is no name, email, or phone number next to it. If someone looked at the database, they would see what was said, but not who said it.

The exception is you. If you type your full name, your school, or your address into a chat, that text is stored with the rest of the message. The tutor doesn't need those details, so please leave them out.

The scrambled network address cannot be turned back into a real address, and we never store the real one.

Who sees what

Three outside services touch your data. Two of them see message content, and one sees only what you choose to report.

  • Anthropic — Claude. Runs the language model that writes the replies. Every message and photo is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. Their terms are at anthropic.com/legal. Anthropic does not train future models on API requests by default.
  • Railway — hosting. Hosts our server and database in the United States. Standard hosting provider; it holds the encrypted traffic and the database that stores conversations.
  • Discord — report notifications only. When you report a reply, a notification with the report's number, reason and which part of the app it came from goes to a private Discord channel that only the developer can read. The reported text itself stays on our server. Nothing else ever goes to Discord.

Anthropic and Railway are the only companies that process message content. We do not share, sell, or transfer your messages to anyone else.

How long we keep it

Deletion is automatic. You don't have to ask for any of these.

What Kept for
Chat and lesson messages 90 days
Photos you attach 24 hours
Content reports 180 days, whether or not we have reviewed them.
Anonymous usage rows 400 days
Lesson progress events 400 days
Lesson completion state As long as the session exists, so progress survives a reinstall
The whole session Erased entirely after 365 days with no activity

Your controls

  • Delete everything from inside the app. Open Settings and tap "Delete my data & start over." This erases everything on our server tied to your session ID and gives you a brand-new ID. From the server's point of view you have never been here before. Available in app version 2.3 and later; on an older version, update the app or use the email option below.
  • Or email us. Write to privacy@trymercurius.com and paste your session ID, which is shown in Settings. We delete the data within 7 days.
  • Deleting the app is not enough. Removing Mercurius from your phone does not delete what is on the server. Use one of the two options above, or wait for the automatic limits in the table.

For parents and schools

There is no account to review and no name on file, so we cannot look up a student by name. What we can tell you: the app is for ages 13 and up, it blocks under-13s at first launch, it collects no contacts or location, it shows no ads, and everything it stores is deleted on the schedule above or on request.

If you want a student's data deleted, ask them to use the button in Settings, or email us their session ID. Questions from a parent or school go to privacy@trymercurius.com and we answer them ourselves.
